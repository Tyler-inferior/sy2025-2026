What our project will do:
Our project will take the scraped data from the website and give you the option to pick a genre. After you pick a genre, it will display the books, along with their prices, ratings, and other information. It will also display it from the best-rated book to the lowest-rated book.


Fields required:
* Title of genre
* Title of books within the genre
* Price of said books
* Star rating 
* Books left in stock
* The UPC
* 1-sentence summary of said books




Definition of done:
1. The app will give you pick the genre and direct you to the books there
2. It will give you the books within the genre in order of star rating
3. It will then let you select the book you want, and will give you the price of the book and how many are left in stock 
4. If you want further information on said book, you can get a 1-sentence summary and the UPC
5. It should then redirect you to the actual site to buy the book
